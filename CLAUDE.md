## What this app is

**50/50** — a Rails web prototype for a mobile app that makes invisible domestic mental load visible. Mission: give women factual data on task distribution in their household.

Full product documentation is at `~/Desktop/50-50-app/`. Before making product decisions, read it — especially `project/02_produit/` and `project/04_tech/`.

**Tone rule (non-negotiable):** the product is feminist in message, universal in execution. Factual and direct. Never soften stats or protect a failing partner from the discomfort of the numbers. Apply the filter: "would we write this for an engaged masculine brand?" — if not, rephrase.

## Core mechanic

- The **household** is the entity that progresses, not individuals.
- Individual contribution is always visible.
- Household progression goes through levels 1–4 then an ultimate level, earned by cumulative **balanced weeks** (1, 3, 6, 10, 15). A week is balanced when every member carries their fair share (100 / members, rounded) ± 10 points of **both** mental load and execution. An unbalanced week only stops progression.
- The household **regresses one level and is blocked** when a member logs nothing for 2 consecutive weeks, unless the other members answer that they were away (a pause is then declared for them). A member can declare as many pauses as they want, and paused weeks don't count. A pause (creation or extension) can never cover a local week where the paused person already has a log, the current week included (error names the next Monday; `AbsenceInquiry#answer_yes!` trims to the empty week or refuses with `member_logged`). Full rules: `docs/backend-backlog.md`.
- Each task log tracks two independent dimensions: **mental load** (plan/organize/decide) and **execution load** (physically doing it).

**Split (lot 2).** `PlannedSplit` (not persisted) computes the 2.2c metric: planned occurrences per week, a shared chore counts in full for each assignee, shares of mental and of execution, one bar = their average (`Share#average`), `exact?` when every bar rounds to the fair share. `SplitProposal` is the D18 algorithm (likes first, dislikes avoided, then balancing toward `targets`, default fair share; lot 6 passes compensated targets). `Household#propose_split!(keep:)` applies it. Pre-join access for an invitee (B10, B11, B14): `SplitAccess#split_household!` and `Household#invitee_participant_for` accept the person who claimed a live invitation (`Invitation#grants_setup_access_to?`); they wait on B12 until `Household#setup_ready_for_invitee?` (the set-up is done), which also drives `Invitation#resume_stage` and `InviteeResume#invitee_resume_path` (B10). B11/B14's last button saves the split with their validation, then calls `Invitation#accept!`, which runs `ChoreAssignee.transfer_invitation!` in the same locked transaction. B10 recomputes a provisional starting split (`WeekPlan#recompute!`). Notifications: `task_assigned` when someone gives a member a chore (`Household#assign_chores!(by:)`, chore form), `monday_split` to the other members when a week plan is proposed, `household_ready` (B12 « Prêt ») to waiting invitees when the set-up ends. Nothing on validation (no matching kind).

**Weekly split (lot 6).** `SplitGap` (`split_gap_entries`) is the carried gap: per member, the sum of real gaps of final weeks (average of the two exact charges minus 100 / active members) since the last reset; formula and worked example at the top of `app/models/split_gap.rb`. `WeeklySplit` (run by `WeeklyEngineJob` after the engine, from Monday 03:00 local once last week is final) folds the gap and, when `weekly_proposal_enabled`, proposes the week through `SplitProposal` towards `SplitGap.targets` (fair share minus balance, kept in the zone), shared chores kept; `monday_split` once per week; `auto_validate_splits` validates at once. Out-of-zone edits save with a warning; the member above the zone may save « sans reporter l'écart » (`week_plans.carry_gap`). C9 reset: `SplitGap.reset!`, only by a member with a positive balance (D4), `gap_reset` to the others. D15/D16: `TaskHandover` (offer for this week, accept/refuse/cancel; `planned_assignee_keys` for the Tasks tab), never changes the plan; notifications `task_offered`, `task_offer_answered`. Live shares: `split-preview` Stimulus controller posts the form to `POST /households/:id/split/preview` (Turbo Stream, nothing saved); « Mettre à jour les parts » stays as the no-JS fallback. D11: `households/splits/_home_card`, rendered in the home slot.

Key relationship: `User → has_many :chores, through: :household` — a user sees all chores of their household, not just personally assigned ones.

**Weekly engine (lot 5a).** Interfaces and rules are documented at the top of each file:
- `WeeklyStats.new(household, week)` (`app/models/weekly_stats.rb`, not persisted): per-member counts and integer % shares of mental and execution logs, `fair_share`, `zone`, `outcome` (`:balanced`/`:unbalanced`/`:neutral`) and `neutral_reason`. The zone judges the exact share (`*_share_exact`, Rational); `*_share` is the rounded display value. The single source of every figure: home screens read it live for the current week, past weeks come from `HouseholdWeek`.
- `HouseholdWeek` (`household_weeks`): snapshot of `WeeklyStats` per household and week (first names and pauses included), `provisional` from Sunday 20:00, `final` from Monday 03:00 local. A final row is `readonly?`: never rewrite it.
- `WeeklyEngine` (`app/services/`), run every 15 min by `WeeklyEngineJob` (`config/recurring.yml`): finals with catch-up, provisional, empty-week streaks (`household_members.empty_weeks_streak`), `AbsenceInquiry` and its Wednesday 20:00 deadline, regression and block, levels (`households.level` 0–5, `balanced_weeks_count`, `state`, `LevelChange` history, `ultimate_reached_on`). Level logic in `Progression`, numbers in `HouseholdRules`.
- `MemberPause` (`member_pauses`, `declared_by_id`): retroactive limits; creating one resolves a pending absence inquiry.
- D3 (decided 2026-11-12): a pause declared after the fact over final weeks never rewrites them; `WeeklyEngine#retroactive_pause_on_final_weeks` lays a `WeekCorrection` over each (`HouseholdWeek#effective_outcome` = neutral, « Neutre · pause déclarée le … »), cancels the regression they caused (`regression_cancelled` LevelChange, level and counter restored, block lifted) and recomputes the streak, within the retroactive-pause window. Ultimate keep rule (decision 2026-11-12): a quota, at most `HouseholdRules::ULTIMATE_UNBALANCED_QUOTA` (7) unbalanced final weeks in the window, neutral and corrected weeks excluded; above it back to level 4, regained automatically once within it. The window runs from `households.ultimate_reached_on`, capped at 52 weeks, and restarts at each reach by thresholds (never at a regain). `Progression.ultimate_margin(household)` = quota − unbalanced weeks, min 0, nil below the ultimate level. A member arriving or leaving (`HouseholdMember` create/destroy, so `leave!` too) resets level, counter and `ultimate_reached_on` (`Household#reset_progression_for_membership_change!`, one `membership_changed` LevelChange, nothing logged when already at zero). Every rule number lives in `HouseholdRules`. Absence answers: `AbsenceInquiry#answer!` / `#correct!` (`absence_answers`, one per member, until Wednesday 20:00 local; one "yes" declares the pause, a correction by the member concerned can withdraw it) and a query API for E11/P5 documented at the top of `absence_inquiry.rb`. `EngineEvent` (`engine_events`) is the outbox notifications read: final and provisional weeks (the final one carries the level right after it), E3 (`member.first_empty_week`), unblocking, every `level.changed`, every `member_pause.declared`, inquiries and answers. `Notifications::Dispatcher.deliver_pending(household)` drains it and marks an event delivered only after the Notifier ran, so a crashed dispatch is replayed without duplicates; `WeeklyEngineJob` calls it after each household of `WeeklyEngine.run_all` (the only loop), and a new pause enqueues `NotificationsDispatchJob`. The weekly report (H3) is built from the final week and goes out Monday 08:00. The screens are lot 5b (below). Not built yet: the Wednesday reminder.

## Data ownership rule (architectural constraint)

Logs belong to the individual. The household is a calculated aggregate. When a user leaves a household, their logs follow them. This is a security/legal decision, not just a design choice — see `~/Desktop/50-50-app/project/04_tech/securite.md`.

**Enforced in the schema.** `chore_logs.chore_id` and `household_id` are nullable with `on_delete: :nullify`; `user_id` is `NOT NULL`. Destroying a chore or a whole household detaches its logs instead of deleting them, and `ChoreLog` snapshots `chore_name`/`category` on create so a detached log still says what was done. Covered by `test/models/chore_log_test.rb`. Do not reintroduce `dependent: :destroy` on either association.

**Departures (lot 7).** Rules at the top of each file:
- `HouseholdMember#leave!` → `HouseholdDeparture` (F2): a chore only the leaver carried goes to every remaining member, a shared one loses them; current and later `WeekPlan`s drop them (`WeekPlan#remove_participant!`, snapshot-based, so D14 holds); their logs keep `user_id` and lose `household_id` and `chore_id`; pending absence inquiries about them and their preferences on the household's chores go; a pending `SplitSettingChange` applies if everyone left agreed (`apply_if_complete!`); the oldest remaining member becomes manager; the level reset comes from the membership callback. Past `household_weeks` are untouched. **The last member out deletes the household.**
- `HouseholdDeletion` (F3, `households#delete` / `#destroy`): manager only, the name typed (spaces squished, case counts); other members get the `household_deleted` notification (event type `alert`, uncapped); live invitations cancelled; logs detached. `notifications.household_id` is nullified, not cascaded, so the message survives.
- `AccountDeletion` (F5, `accounts#delete_account`, then `DELETE /users` → `Users::RegistrationsController#destroy`): password + ticked confirmation; leaves the household first; **`dispose_of_logs!` is the one switch for the logs** (`LOGS_ON_DELETION = :delete`, product owner decision 2026-11-12; `:keep_anonymised` keeps them on the anonymised account); drops preferences, notifications and their settings, approvals; rewrites the first name to « Ancien membre » in every JSON snapshot that names the person (`household_weeks`, `week_plans`, others' `notifications`, `engine_events`, `week_corrections`), figures unchanged; the `users` row stays (`deleted_at`, unroutable email, random password, sign-in refused).

## Back office (`/admin`)

ActiveAdmin 4.0.0.beta23 (`app/admin/`), read-only: accounts, households, invitations, audit log, dashboard. Never shown: logs, preferences, per-member shares, gap entries, notification contents (a test checks that no admin route mentions them). Writes go through `BackOffice::Actions` only (resend or cancel an invitation, unblock a household, confirm an email, delete an account), each logged in `admin_actions`. Access: `BackOfficeGate` (a signed-in `AdminUser` whose sign-in gave a TOTP code). First admin: `ADMIN_EMAIL=you@x bin/rails admin:bootstrap` (password asked on the terminal; ENV `ADMIN_PASSWORD` only as a warned fallback). It prints a one-time enrolment link (30 min): the TOTP secret is shown only to a session that opened it and signed in. `admin:reset_two_factor` issues a new one and ends the admin's sessions. Sessions: 30 min idle, 8 h absolute. Sorting is limited to each resource's `sortable_by` list (`BackOfficeSortable`). `admin_actions` is append-only in Postgres (trigger), so the schema is dumped as `db/structure.sql`, not `schema.rb`. TOTP secrets need Active Record encryption keys in production (`config/initializers/active_record_encryption.rb`).

CSS: the tokens are in `app/assets/tailwind/tokens.css`, imported by `application.css` and by the admin build. `bin/rails tailwindcss:build` also runs `active_admin:css` (`lib/tasks/active_admin_assets.rake`: standalone CLI, no Node; it makes the gem's plugin.js loadable and writes `tmp/active_admin/`). `bin/dev` only watches the app CSS: re-run `bin/rails active_admin:css` after changing `active_admin.css`.
